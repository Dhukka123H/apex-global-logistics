About Apex Global Logistics

A fictional enterprise built for an academic demonstration of OSINT concepts and information exposure risks.

Moving Information. Connecting Possibilities.

Apex Global Logistics (AGL) is a fictional logistics company concept. Its sample website illustrates how an organization might present its corporate profile, services, leadership structure, and downloadable business documents.

This project demonstrates how publicly accessible pages and document properties can reveal organizational details. The names, company information, contact addresses, and business scenarios displayed here are synthetic and are not intended to represent real individuals or an operating business.

The project supports cybersecurity education by helping students understand information exposure, document metadata, reconnaissance techniques, and practical defensive controls.
